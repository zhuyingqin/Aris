# Research checkpoint

Record these facts using existing project goal and session storage:

1. Current objective, milestone and observable success criteria.
2. Verified evidence, its local artifact path and what it supports.
3. Unverified progress, missing evidence and contradictory results.
4. Independent Reviewer findings and unresolved revision requests.
5. Next executable action, its inputs and its expected output.

Do not invent completed experiments, citations or Reviewer approval. Preserve
earlier evidence and user-authored project guidance.
