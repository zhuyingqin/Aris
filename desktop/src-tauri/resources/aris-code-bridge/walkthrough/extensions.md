## Extensions

The marketplace here is [Open VSX](https://open-vsx.org), the vendor-neutral
registry. Language servers, linters, formatters, keymaps and themes are almost
all published there.

A few Microsoft-licensed extensions are not, and never will be — Pylance,
the C/C++ tools, C#, and Remote-SSH among them. For Python, `basedpyright` or
Jedi cover most of what Pylance does.

Themes are the one thing worth leaving alone at first: SomniQ pushes its own
palette so the editor matches the rest of the app. Picking a theme yourself
overrides that, which is fine — it is your editor.
